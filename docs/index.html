Features

Everything you need for an epic block-building adventure.

🧱

150 Unique Blocks

From classic grass and stone to deepslate, copper variants, cherry wood, bamboo, sculk, and ancient debris.

🎮

3D Raycasting Mode

Wolfenstein-style first-person rendering. Press F9 to toggle between 2D platformer and immersive 3D worlds.

🥽

VR Side-by-Side

Press F10 for stereoscopic split-screen. Works with cardboard VR headsets and cross-eyed viewing.

🌐

Cross-Platform Multiplayer

Host LAN servers with auto-discovery. Android and desktop players can join the same world seamlessly.

🎙️

In-Game Voice Chat

UDP voice chat on server port + 1000. Talk to friends while you build, no third-party apps needed.

🎵

Discord Rich Presence

Party invites, join secrets, and real-time status via HTTP bridge. Bring your Discord friends in.

⚔️

Survival & Creative

Toggle modes with F key. Hunger, health, mobs, crafting table, furnace smelting, and 15+ recipes.

🔥

Fire & Physics

Spreading fire, soul fire, TNT explosions, sand physics, superflat world toggle, and Nether portals.
